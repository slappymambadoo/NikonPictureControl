Nikon Picture Control Comparison

The same scene rendered through every Nikon Picture Control, one JPEG per preset. Three scenes so you can judge how each look holds up across different light. Includes Nikon's built-in controls (the numbered and lettered ones, e.g. [01] Dream, [SD] Standard) plus a large set of custom and downloaded ones. Click any preset for a full-size view; use the strip at the bottom of the viewer to compare the same preset across scenes.
